# NEON privacy policy

NEON has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

- **On your device only:** high scores, favorites, settings and the games you create are saved in the app's local storage. They never leave the device. *Settings → Reset all data* deletes them.
- **Online mode (optional):** to connect two phones, NEON uses the free open-source PeerJS service to introduce the two devices, then they talk directly (WebRTC). Only game frames, sounds and touch input pass between the two phones while you play. Nothing is stored. Other modes never use the internet.
- **Share codes** contain only a game's settings (name, game type, colors, speed, size, effects), and only go where you paste them.
- **Permissions (Android):** Internet (online mode only) and Vibrate (haptics).

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
