# NEON privacy policy

NEON collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and it never connects to the internet
(the Android app does not even have the Internet permission).

- **On your device only:** scores, play time, saved levels, streaks, trophies, favourites, settings and the games you
  create are stored in the app's local storage. *Settings → Reset all data* deletes them. Uninstalling deletes them too.
- **Reminders** are planned and shown by the phone itself (Android alarms and notifications). You can turn them off in
  Settings or in Android's notification settings.
- **Share codes** contain only a game's settings (name, game type, colours, speed, size, effects) and only go where you
  paste them.
- **Permissions (Android):** notifications (reminders), run at start-up (to keep reminders after a restart), vibrate.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
