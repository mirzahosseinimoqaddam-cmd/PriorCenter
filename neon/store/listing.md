# NEON — store listing

**Short description (80):** 370 glowing neon games in one app. Play offline, keep streaks, make your own.

**Full description:**

NEON is an arcade in your pocket: 370 games drawn in glowing neon lines, all in one small app that works completely offline.

• 70 kinds of game: pinball, sudoku, pool, air hockey, ball sort, tunnel runner, pipes, picross, bowling, snake, blocks, slice, invaders and many more, each with several versions.
• One tap to play. Short games for a bus ride, puzzles with saved levels for longer sessions.
• Daily challenge, streaks, levels, 24 trophies and new games that unlock as you play.
• Your stats: play time per game, best scores, saved levels and a weekly chart.
• Gentle smart reminders at the time you usually play, and only when it matters (for example, to keep your streak).
• Play against the computer on easy, normal or hard, or with a friend on the same phone.
• Make your own games: mix two games, change speed, size, colours and add effects, then share them with a code.
• English and Persian. Colour-blind friendly colours, large text and reduced-motion options.
• No ads. No accounts. No tracking. No internet needed.
